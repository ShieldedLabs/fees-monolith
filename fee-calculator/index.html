The algorithm in 60 seconds

1
Look back 50 blocks (skipping the 5 most recent for stability). For every non-coinbase transaction, compute fee / max(2, logical_actions).
2
Fill unused capacity with synthetic entries. For each block, the unused bytes (out of 2 MB) are filled with fake transactions priced at the floor: 1,000 zats/action. This pulls the median toward the floor when the network is quiet.
3
Take the median, bucket to the nearest power of 10. Ties round down. The result is the standard_fee. The floor (1,000 zats) is enforced as a minimum.
4
Detect congestion. If zero synthetic entries were added (every block in the window was full), the network is congested. An express_fee of 10x the standard fee becomes available.

Where this is going

Phase 1: z_getstandardfees

March – April 2026

RPC endpoint that publishes fee data from confirmed blocks. Implemented in Zebra, relayed by Zaino and lightwalletd. You're looking at the result.

Phase 2: Earthquake Tests

May – August 2026

Stress-test under adversarial conditions. Replay harness, wallet developer outreach, relay and mining policy updates. Must pass experiment gates E2–E5, E7.

Phase 3: Consensus Building

September 2026 –

Feature-flagged PRs in Zebra, Zaino, and lightwalletd. ZIP advancement to "Proposed" status. Wallet integration. Policy convergence gate: >85% for 4 consecutive windows.

Phase 4: Extended Research

Ongoing

Monitor, iterate, run extended research gates E8–E12 covering learning robustness, collusion frontier, resource-basis ablation, and controller benchmarks.
