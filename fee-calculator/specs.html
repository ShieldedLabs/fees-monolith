The drafts behind the numbers

Two ZIP drafts underpin this work: the z_getstandardfee estimator and a ZIP-317 marginal-fee reduction. Both are Draft, unassigned, not yet on zips.z.cash. The full text of each is hosted below; discussion is on the Zcash community forum. If you build transactions, start with the integrator quick-start.

For integrators

Integrate dynamic fees

For anything that builds Zcash transactions: wallets, but also DEXs, bridges, and intents or solver systems. Two things to add now, one to adopt when the estimator ships.

Now: two features

  • A Priority lane. Offer a higher-fee option and label it exactly "Priority." The shared name matters: if wallets each call it something different, the choice starts segmenting users by wording. Under current ZIP-317 parameters the priority fee is 4× the standard fee.
  • Speed-up semantics. Set a short transaction expiry, and when a transaction expires, resubmit it at the priority rate. This is the "bump a stuck transaction" experience without replace-by-fee, which Zcash deliberately does not do.

Next: use the estimator

When z_getstandardfee is available from your indexer (Zaino over JSON-RPC, lightwalletd over gRPC), call it to set the standard and priority numbers from live network data instead of hard-coding them. Until then, use the current ZIP-317 conventional fee. The endpoint is specified in the estimator ZIP below.

Pilot coordination and integration questions: forum.zcashcommunity.com/c/technology/dynamic-fees

Draft Unassigned Standards / RPC

Dynamic Fee Estimation via z_getstandardfee

Owner
Mark Henderson
Updates
New RPC (no consensus change)
Created
2026-03-16
Discussion
forum.zcashcommunity.com/c/technology/dynamic-fees

Specifies z_getstandardfee, a full-node RPC (computed by Zebra, relayed by Zaino and lightwalletd) that publishes a fee recommendation derived from confirmed blocks. The result is observational: it reflects what the network recently accepted, not a guarantee of future inclusion. No consensus change; wallets adopt it as a policy recommendation. The minimal v0 response is three fields: standard_fee, version, and height.

The v0 reference algorithm

  • Look back 50 blocks, buffered by 5 from the tip (reorg margin). For every non-coinbase transaction, compute fee / max(grace_actions, logical_actions).
  • Synthetic fill: model unused block capacity (out of 2 MB) as floor-fee transactions at 1,000 zats/action, so the median has a stable signal even when the network is quiet.
  • Take the median of the combined multiset, then round to the nearest power of 10; the floor is enforced as the minimum.
  • Versioned: any change to parameters or formula increments version, and a new conformance-vector set is published.

Open v0 choices

Three parameters are fixed in v0 but open to optional refinement if observed behavior warrants: the oracle (action-weighted median vs. median-of-medians), the floor (1,000 vs. 5,000 zats), and the quantization (powers of 10 vs. alternatives). See the design page and research.

Privacy, and the cost of a fee choice

Powers-of-10 bucketing keeps fee entropy low, and the estimator reads only confirmed blocks, never the mempool. The draft also sketches (without specifying) a future direction directly relevant to the priority-usage question: the anonymity set of a fee choice, the number of recent confirmed actions that paid the same fee. A tier paid by many blends in; one paid by few stands out. A future estimator version could expose tier occupancy so a wallet can warn before a user sends into a sparsely-populated lane.

Draft Unassigned Standards / Wallet

Reduce Marginal Fee to 1,000 Zatoshis

Owner
Mark Henderson
Updates
ZIP 317 (wallet convention, no consensus change)
Created
2026-04-06
Discussion
forum.zcashcommunity.com/c/technology/dynamic-fees

Reduces ZIP-317's marginal_fee from 5,000 to 1,000 zats per logical action, dropping the minimum 2-action transaction fee from 10,000 to 2,000 zats. ZIP-317 was calibrated in 2022 near $30 ZEC; as the price rose, the fixed fee grew in fiat terms with no way to adjust. The new value restores the fiat cost to near the original design point and aligns with a powers-of-10 fee alphabet. All other ZIP-317 parameters and formulae are unchanged.

  • Policy-only. ZIP-317 fees are a wallet convention, so no network upgrade is required. Users keep the ability to override.
  • Safe by defense-in-depth. The marginal fee is one layer; block_unpaid_action_limit (0 on Zebra today), ZIP-401 mempool eviction, and the ZIP-235 burn are independent of its level.
  • ZIP-235 interaction. At the new 2,000-zat minimum, 1,200 zats are burned and 800 paid to the miner.
  • Sunset clause. Expires 12 months after Active status, reverting to 5,000 unless renewed or superseded by an active dynamic-fee mechanism. The sunset forces re-evaluation, the very failure mode that motivated the change.
Note. An earlier idea to also raise ZIP-317's weight-ratio cap from 4 to 10 has been deprioritized: priority ships at 4× under current parameters, and the cap change is a possible later refinement, not part of this draft. See the design page.
